Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 25% of its height on every side. Don't place text, other logos, or busy imagery inside that zone.

Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the favicon-optimized files.

Color

Warm paper, forest ink, a spark of amber.

Four core colors carry the whole identity. Click any swatch to copy its hex value.

Pairing rules: forest on paper, amber on ink. Never set amber type on paper (fails contrast) or forest on ink at small sizes.

Usage

A few rules keep the flame recognizable.

✓ Do

  • Use the files on this page as provided.
  • Write the name as eCandle — lowercase e, capital C.
  • Pick the variant made for your background.
  • Keep the 25% clear-space zone.
  • Credit as “eCandle · Built on Arkreen” where context matters.

✕ Don't

  • Recolor, add gradients, outlines, or shadows to the mark.
  • Stretch, rotate, or redraw any part of it.
  • Place the mark on busy imagery without a mono variant.
  • Combine it with other logos into a new lockup.
  • Use the mark to imply endorsement without a partnership.
