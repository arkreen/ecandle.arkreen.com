Questions before you order.

What can the 300W Solar Kit power?

It is designed for household essentials such as efficient lights, a fan, television and phone charging, provided the combined load remains within the system's continuous output limit.

What can the 500W Solar Kit power?

It adds output for a small shop, phone-charging service, shop lighting, mobile-money operation and a compatible small efficient refrigerator.

Can the 500W kit run any refrigerator?

No. Refrigerator running power and startup surge vary by model. Share the appliance nameplate or model with the local partner so compatibility can be confirmed before purchase.

What is included in the Solar Kit?

The standard offer includes the eCandle power station, matched solar panel, standard packaging, AC charging cable, device identity / PAYGo interface and a basic product test. Your final order must confirm the exact cable and installation scope.

Are delivery and installation included?

They are confirmed separately based on delivery location, mounting, wiring and site requirements.

How does PAYGo work?

PAYGo-ready control allows a participating local program to authorize energy access according to purchase, installment, rental or shared-use rules. Program availability and payment methods vary by partner and location.

Where can I find detailed technical specifications?
