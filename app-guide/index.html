App field guide

Using the eCandle app.

For customers, agents and operators in the pilot. Android only for now. Button names are written exactly as they appear in the app.

Install Pay for power Bluetooth Cash Problems Operators

Everyone

Install the app

  1. Open the install link you were sent, in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Download the file, then open it.
  3. If Android says the install is blocked, tap Settings, allow installs from Chrome, go back and tap Install.

When a new version is sent to you, install it the same way. It replaces the old one and keeps your payment history.

Customers

Pay for power

You do not need an account.

  1. Tap Scan device code and point the camera at the code on the device. No camera? Type the code printed on the device, for example KAN-1, and tap Go.
  2. Choose how long you want power: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour or 2 hours. The price appears under You pay.
  3. Tap Pay. The payment page opens. Pay with your wallet, then come back to the app.
  4. Wait on the Payment screen. It updates by itself, usually in under a minute.
Device with internetThe screen shows Power is on and counts down the time you bought.
Device without internetThe screen shows One more step. Send the credit over Bluetooth, below.

Customers

Send the credit over Bluetooth

Some devices have no internet. Your phone carries the credit to the device.

  1. Stand next to the device. Bluetooth must be on.
  2. Tap Send credit over Bluetooth, then Find the device. The first time, Android asks for permission to find nearby devices. Tap Allow.
  3. A list appears. Tap the name that starts with PAYGO-. The closest device is listed first.
  4. When the screen shows the device and your credit, tap Send 1 credit.
  5. You are done when you see All credit delivered and the time starts counting down.
Your credit is safe

It stays on the server until the device receives it. Closing the app, losing signal or a flat battery does not lose it. Come back to the device, open My payments, open the order and send it again.

Unplugging the device pauses the countdown. You do not lose time you paid for.

Customers

Pay with cash

  1. Choose your time and tap Pay, but do not pay on the payment page. Come back to the app.
  2. On the Payment screen tap Pay cash to an agent. An 8-character code appears.
  3. Show the code to your local eCandle agent and pay them. They confirm it on their phone and your screen changes by itself.

Customers

If something goes wrong

Device not found
Check the code and scan again.
Payment timed out
You did not pay within 10 minutes. Tap Try again. If you did pay, keep the screen open. Paid orders are recovered automatically.
Bluetooth is turned off
Tap Open Bluetooth settings, turn it on, come back and tap Find the device.
No device found
Make sure the device is switched on and you are within a few metres. Tap Search again.
Wrong device
You connected to a different device from the one you paid for. Tap Choose another device. Nothing was sent.
The connection to the device was lost
Move closer and tap Find the device again. Your credit is safe.
No response from the device
Move closer and send again. Nothing was lost.
Too many failed attempts, the device is locked
Wait for the time shown, then try again.
The power did not come on
Open the device again and tap Report a problem.

Agents and operators

The operator console

Tap Operator sign-in at the bottom of the home screen and sign in with your operator account. You only see your own devices, orders and tickets. You stay signed in until you tap Sign out.

Confirm a cash payment

  1. The customer shows you the 8-character code on their phone.
  2. Open Cash payments and type the code in the search box.
  3. Check the amount and the device. Take the cash.
  4. Tap the order, then Yes, I received $…

Only confirm after you have the cash in your hand. Confirming gives the customer their power and records the amount against your account.

If the app says the order was already paid through another channel, do not take cash for it. The customer has already paid online.

Check your devices

My devices lists every device with its status and battery level. Offline devices are at the top. Tap a device to see battery, power in and out, and WiFi signal. Pull down to refresh.

A device that works over Bluetooth may have no internet. It then shows Offline here even though it is working. That is normal.

See payments

Payments lists every payment on your devices. Filter with Paid, Waiting, Expired or All. Tap a payment to open the transaction on the block explorer. Cash payments show cash.

Handle tickets

Customers report problems from the device screen. The reports arrive under Tickets.

  1. Open holds new reports. Tap Start when you begin working on one.
  2. Under In progress, tap Mark as done…, write what you did, then tap Mark as done.
  3. Done keeps finished reports with your note.

If the customer left a phone number or email, tap Contact to call or write to them. A report marked Overdue has waited too long. Handle it first.

Everyone

Getting help

Send the eCandle team the device code, what you tapped, and a screenshot of the screen. Include the lines of small text at the bottom of the Bluetooth screen. They tell us exactly what the device answered.
