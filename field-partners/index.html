Before you apply

Who can apply?

Individuals and teams running a site anywhere in Africa can propose a pilot: a community space, a shop or a campus hub.

How many units, and for how long?

Selected partners receive one or two units for eight weeks, counted from the day you first use them.

Who pays local charges?

Arkreen arranges sea freight. Both teams agree who covers local charges and delivery before shipment.

What happens to the units after the trial?

Both teams agree on this before anything ships, so there are no surprises at the end of the eight weeks.

Do I need to send a video?

No. A few lines about your site are enough to apply. You can decide later whether to share photos or a field story.

Interviews, photos and public sharing are asked for separately if your site is selected, and never affect whether you’re shortlisted.
